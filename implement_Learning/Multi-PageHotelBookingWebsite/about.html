About ABC Hotel

A place to slow down

ABC Hotel is a family-run mountain retreat focused on thoughtful hospitality, comfortable rooms, and a close connection to the surrounding community.

We are proud to welcome guests throughout the year. Our team is happy to share local recommendations and help make each visit feel personal.

What matters to us

  • Warm, attentive service
  • Fresh food prepared with local ingredients
  • Respect for the landscape and neighboring community

Our story

Since opening in 2008, we have helped guests discover the trails, views, and traditions of this region. We follow the STA guidelines for responsible local tourism.
