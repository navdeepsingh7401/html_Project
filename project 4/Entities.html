HTML Entities

HTML entities are used to display special characters that are normally reserved in HTML.

Common Characters

  • & for ampersand: A & B
  • < for less than: 5 < 10
  • > for greater than: 10 > 5
  • " for quotation marks: "Hello"
  • © for copyright: © 2026
<li>&amp; for ampersand: A & B</li>
<li>&lt; for less than: 5 &lt; 10</li>
<li>&copy; for copyright: &copy; 2026</li>

Why are entities useful?

Some characters like <, >, and & are treated specially by HTML. Entities help display them correctly.

Example
