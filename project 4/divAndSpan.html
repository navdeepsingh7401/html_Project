div and span

What is <div>?

The <div> tag is a block-level container used to group large sections of content.

Example of div

This content is inside a div block.

<div>
  <h3>Example of div</h3>
  <p>This content is inside a div block.</p>
</div>

What is <span>?

The <span> tag is an inline container used for styling or marking small portions of text.

This is a highlighted word inside a sentence.

<p>This is a <span style="color: blue; font-weight: bold;">highlighted word</span> inside a sentence.</p>

Difference

  • div is block-level and takes full width.
  • span is inline and only wraps the text it contains.
